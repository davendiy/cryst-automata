The matrices $\mathcal{X}$ satisfying the condition PC have one of 24 possible forms, so we omit the full list for
the sake of simplicity. Similarly, $G_{220}$ is not symmorphic, instead one should seek for $G_{217}$ in the Table
\ref{tab:norm_dim3_102}, which has the same point group.

Consider matrix A=$ \left(\begin{array}{rrr}
-x_{0} & 0 & -2 \, x_{0} \\
0 & -x_{0} & x_{0} \\
0 & 0 & x_{0}
\end{array}\right) $.
The point group also has 24 elements, so both congruence systems (\ref{eqn:cocycle_condition}) and (\ref{eqn:small_cong_system})
are too big to include. Instead, here is the one conjugation for which there should be given some comments:
\begin{footnotesize}
$$(A, a)\left(\begin{array}{rrr|r}
1 & 0 & 0 & -\frac{1}{2} \\
-1 & 0 & -1 & \frac{1}{2} \\
-1 & -1 & 0 & 1 \\
\end{array}\right)(A^{-1}, -A^{-1}a) = 
\left(\begin{array}{rrr|r}
-1 & -2 & 0 & 2 \, a_{0} + 2 \, a_{1} - \frac{3}{2} \, x_{0} \\
0 & 1 & 0 & \frac{1}{2} \, x_{0} \\
1 & 1 & 1 & -a_{0} - a_{1} + x_{0} \\
\end{array}\right) \equiv
\left(\begin{array}{rrr|r}
-1 & -2 & 0 & \frac{1}{2} \\
0 & 1 & 0 & -\frac{1}{2} \\
1 & 1 & 1 & -\frac{1}{2} \\
\end{array}\right) \mod 1$$
\end{footnotesize}
It is a rare case when we can not simply fix a trivial translation $a = 0 \in \mathbb{Z}^3$, as it leads to a
contradiction $\frac{1}{2}x_0 \equiv -\frac{1}{2}$ while simultaneously $x \equiv -\frac{1}{2}$.

After solving the system of congruences for the CC condition, we get
$$A' = \left(\begin{array}{rrr}
-2 \, y_{0} + 3 & 0 & -4 \, y_{0} + 6 \\
0 & -2 \, y_{0} + 3 & 2 \, y_{0} - 3 \\
0 & 0 & 2 \, y_{0} - 3
\end{array}\right).$$
$A'$ falls into the case 1, and the respective triple of polynomials are (can be found in Table \ref{tab:case1_srdegrees286}):
$$ p = 2y_0 - 3, \quad q = 4y_0^2 - 12y_{0} + 9, \quad g = -4y_0 + 6 $$
The solution to the condition on eigenvalues $(q\pm g) \neq -1$ is a finite set of roots $y_0 \notin \{1, 2\}$, which
combining with $|p| \neq \pm 1$ gives us:
$$
SRD = \left\{ (2y_0 - 3)(4y_0^2 - 12y_{0} + 9) \, : \, y_0 \notin \{1, 2\} \right\} = \left\{ (2y_0 - 3)^3 \, : \, y_0 \notin \{1, 2\} \right\}
$$
which is all perfect cubes greater than 1.
